The Data Mining Pipeline

The Journey Ahead
Raw Data → Cleaning → Modelling → Insight

Welcome to Module 2! Before we write a single line of code, let's look at the big picture. Data Mining isn't just about throwing algorithms at numbers—it is a strict, structured pipeline. If you skip a step, your model will fail.

2.1 Explore Data → 2.2 Preprocess → 2.3 Model & Evaluate → 2.4 Visualize (PCA)

The Story of a Data Point

Before diving into the code, let's visualize the entire pipeline. Think of a raw data point as an unrefined ingredient arriving at a high-end restaurant.

1. The Blueprint Before touching data, we must understand the end goal. What problem are we solving?
2. The Delivery (Pandas) Raw, messy data arrives at the loading dock. We use Pandas to inspect and wrangle it.
3. The Vault (Train/Test Split) We lock away 20% of our data in a vault to test our final model on unseen data. No peeking!
4. The Survivor (Imputation) Missing values are gaps in our knowledge. We fill them in with educated guesses (mean/median).
5. The Chopping Block Useless columns and rare outliers are chopped away to reduce noise.
6. The Scale (Normalization) We shrink massive numbers so they don't overpower the small ones. Everything must be on equal footing.
7. The Blender (OHE) Models only understand math. We blend words and categories into 1s and 0s using One-Hot Encoding.
8. The Master Chef (Modelling) The Logistic Regression algorithm learns the patterns from our perfectly prepped data.
9. The Critic (Evaluation) We unlock the Test Data from the vault and grade our model's performance using ROC-AUC.
10. The Grand Feast (Insight) We use PCA to visualize the results and deliver actionable insights.

The 4-Part Journey

This module is broken down into four distinct steps. We will follow a real dataset (The Titanic) from raw, messy data all the way to a finished, evaluated machine learning model.

Step 1

Pandas & EDA

Before doing anything, you must understand your data. We'll learn how to load, inspect, and slice data using Pandas.

 Step 2

Data Preprocessing

Real data is messy. We'll learn how to properly split data, fill missing holes (imputation), convert words to numbers (OHE), and scale it.

 Step 3

Modelling & Evaluation

With clean data, we will train our very first Logistic Regression model and learn how to grade it like a harsh food critic.

 Step 4

PCA & t-SNE

What happens when data has thousands of columns instead of just a few? We squash it using advanced dimensionality reduction techniques.
